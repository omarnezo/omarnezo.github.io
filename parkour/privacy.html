Privacy Policy – Sky Hop Parkour

Developer: thebogaman · App ID: com.thebogaman.parkour · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Sky Hop Parkour ("the game") is a 3D parkour game for players of all ages. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show those ads, as explained below.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: stages reached, best times, coins, hats you unlocked, your character's look, sound settings and the age range you chose. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it. Device backups to the cloud are turned off for this game.

2. Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no multiplayer, no in-app purchases and no analytics of our own. We do not have a server that receives information from the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner in Rage Tower, and optional videos that you can choose to watch for a reward (extra lives, a second chance, or double coins). To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • the device's advertising ID or other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic or crash data related to ads.

This information is handled by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children and the age question

When the game starts for the first time, it asks the player's age. The answer stays on the device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads:

AgeAds shown
Under 13Ad requests are tagged as child-directed (COPPA). Only ads rated for general audiences (G) are shown, and ads are never personalized or based on interests.
13 to 15Ad requests are tagged as from a user under the age of consent. Ads are never personalized.
16 and olderStandard ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game never asks for a name, email, photo, location or contacts. On iPhone, the game does not ask for permission to track.

5. Your choices

  • You can reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Watching reward videos is always optional.
  • The game can be played offline. Without internet, no ads are loaded.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all data stored by the game on your device.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
